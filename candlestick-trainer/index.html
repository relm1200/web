Reading candles on forex charts

  • Gaps are rare. Spot FX trades around the clock from Sunday evening to Friday, so each candle usually opens at the previous close. Stars, piercing lines and dark clouds are judged on bodies and closes, not gaps.
  • Higher timeframes are cleaner. H4 and daily candles filter out intraday noise. The daily candle most brokers use closes at 17:00 New York time.
  • Location matters more than shape. A hammer at a tested support level or round number (1.0800, 150.00) is worth far more than one in the middle of a range.
  • Wait for the close. A candle isn't a pattern until it closes. A hammer half-way through the session can still finish as a bearish candle.
  • Plan the risk first. Most patterns give a natural stop beyond the wick extreme. Size the position from that distance, not the other way round.
